# Donations → Providers

A **provider** accepts a player's coin purchase. KaevCMS credits the player's wallet only after verifying payment.

## Connect a provider

1. Open **Donations → Payment systems** in administration.
2. Choose the provider and click **Configure**.
3. Enter account details and configure notifications.

## Guides

- **[YooMoney — connect and verify with the Test button](/en/guide/donations-yoomoney)**

KaevCMS generates the YooMoney notification URL automatically. After it receives a correctly signed test notification, it saves the settings for you.
