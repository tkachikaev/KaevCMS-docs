# Support Tickets

**Support Tickets** lets players contact site staff from their account. Staff can reply, update statuses, assign tickets and add internal notes.

All data stays in the **KaevCMS database**; no game-database connection is needed.

## Module guides

- [Support settings](/en/guide/support-tickets-settings) — permissions, limits and retention.
- [Managing tickets](/en/guide/support-tickets-requests) — viewing, replying and statuses.

## Enable the module

Open **Modules**, enable **Support Tickets**, then visit its administration section and review the settings.

[Technical documentation on GitHub](https://github.com/tkachikaev/KaevCMS/blob/main/modules/support-tickets/docs/README.en.md).
